    Common questions

    Everything parents want to know.

    Still wondering how Parentee fits into your family? These are the questions we hear most.

    What is Parentee?

    Parentee is an AI parenting companion that gives you personalised, judgment-free guidance for every stage of raising a child. Unlike a generic chatbot, Parentee remembers each child's age, allergies, routines and temperament so every answer is tailored to your family, not a one-size-fits-all forum post.

    How is Parentee different from Google or ChatGPT?

    Search engines return hundreds of conflicting links; ChatGPT gives generic answers that know nothing about your child. Parentee knows your child's name, age, quirks and history. Ask "what should Mia eat this week?" and it factors in her picky-eating stage, any intolerances you've shared, and meals you've already tried. Personalised parenting advice, not population-average advice.

    What kinds of questions can I ask Parentee?

    Almost anything about raising your children. Parents commonly ask about sleep schedules, picky eating, meal ideas, sibling conflicts, tantrums, screen time boundaries, school and homework struggles, writing teacher emails, developmental milestones and daily routines. If it's one of the hundred small questions a day that don't warrant a doctor's appointment, Parentee is here for it.

    What ages does Parentee support?

    Parentee supports children from newborn through to 17 years old. Whether you're managing a newborn's feeding schedule, navigating toddler tantrums, or talking to a twelve-year-old about peer pressure, Parentee adapts its tone and guidance to match your child's developmental stage.

    Can Parentee help with tantrums, sleep or behaviour problems?

    Yes. The Behaviour Coaching feature offers gentle, evidence-informed strategies for tantrums, meltdowns, back-talk, sleep resistance, sibling fights and screen time battles. Advice is tailored to your child's temperament and age, not generic tips that may not fit your situation.

    Can I use Parentee for more than one child?

    Yes. The Family plan supports up to 4 children, each with their own remembered profile. The Family+ plan extends this to 6 children and adds a co-parent invite so both parents share the same family account. A question about your 8-year-old never gets confused with advice for your toddler.

    Does Parentee replace my doctor or paediatrician?

    No. Parentee is a parenting companion, not a medical professional. For medical, mental-health or developmental concerns, always consult a qualified healthcare provider. Parentee is here for everyday questions, routines and decisions, not clinical diagnosis or treatment.

    How does Parentee protect my family's data and privacy?

    Your family's data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Parentee does not sell your data or use your children's information to train AI models. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. We are committed to GDPR and COPPA compliance, and never store sensitive information without your explicit consent.

    How much does Parentee cost? Is there a free plan?

    Parentee has a free Starter plan for one child. The Family plan is $7.42 per month (billed $89 annually) and covers up to 4 children with unlimited questions, meal plans, grocery lists and school email drafts. Family+ at $13.25 per month adds 6 children, a co-parent invite and a birthday planner. All paid plans start with a free trial.
